Worth noticing, not yet proving Pages 2 and 3 are each shorter than chunk_size (495 and 473 characters) — yet both still produce 2 chunks, because chunk_text() never checks whether there's meaningful new content left; it just keeps slicing while start < len(text). Their second chunk ends up entirely contained inside their first — pure redundancy from a loop that doesn't know when to stop early. And every chunk's id is just str(i), a position counter that only stays unique because this is one document, one in-memory run.
