Where exactly do the "first 5 values" come from?

vec is a 768-element list. vec[:5] is a plain Python slice — index 0 up to (not including) index 5. Nothing is computed or rounded for the printout; it's the first five raw floats, in the order Ollama returned them.

Worth noticing, not yet proving Sentence 1 and 2 share zero words but sit in a similar numeric range across all five sample values; sentence 3 drifts further, especially in dimensions 3 and 5. That's a hint, not a measurement — five numbers out of 768 can't tell you "similar" or "different" on their own. Turning that hint into one comparable score is exactly what Step 2 — Cosine Similarity does next.
